## Taxonomy and environments / 分类与生境

Accepted fish species, scientific names, authorities, higher taxonomy (class, order, family, genus), environments (freshwater, marine, brackish), extinction flags, and COL IDs come from **Catalogue of Life** (Version 2026-11-11, ChecklistBank dataset 317005; fish sector contributed by FishBase 2026-11-01), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

现生鱼类接受种、学名、命名人、分类（纲/目/科/属）、生境标记（淡水/海水/半咸水）、灭绝标记及 COL 编号来自 **Catalogue of Life（2026-11-11 版）**，许可为 [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)。

## Names, external IDs, and IUCN status / 俗名、外部编号与保护等级

Multilingual common names (`zh-Hans`, `zh-Hant`, `en`, `ja`, `ko`, `fr`, `de`, `es`, `pt`, `ru`), Chinese Wikipedia links, external database identifiers (FishBase SpecCode, WoRMS AphiaID, GBIF taxonKey, iNaturalist taxon ID), and IUCN conservation status codes (property P141) come from **Wikidata**, dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

多语种鱼名、中文维基百科链接、外部数据库编号（FishBase、WoRMS、GBIF、iNaturalist）及 IUCN 保护等级（P141）来自 **Wikidata**，采用 [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) 释放至公有领域。

## Regional occurrence tagging / 区域分布标记

China occurrence flags (`inChina`) are derived from **GBIF** species occurrence facets (`country=CN`).

中国分布标记（`inChina`）基于 **GBIF** 物种记录统计生成。

## Reference photographs / 参考照片

Reference photographs are **not** included in this repository. Each sticker's reference source page, photographer (where known), and license are recorded in `sticker.reference`. If your photograph was used as a reference and you prefer it not to be, please open a [takedown request](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/takedown.yml) and we will replace the sticker within 7 days.

本仓库**不包含**任何参考照片。每张贴纸的参考照出处、作者和授权均记录在 `sticker.reference` 中。如不希望您的照片被用作参考，请提交[撤图申请](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/takedown.yml)，我们将在 7 天内替换。
